Before you go

What's included, and what to bring

Show up with sunscreen and snacks. We'll handle everything else.

We provide

  • Bait
  • Rods & reels
  • Tackle
  • Fishing licenses
  • Ice & cooler
  • Water
  • Fuel
  • Local knowledge

You bring

  • Hat
  • Polarized sunglasses
  • Lunch or snacks
  • Drinks (no glass, please)
  • Reef-safe sunscreen

Non-aerosol sunscreen, please. Aerosol sprays ruin boat cushions and leave the deck and handholds slippery, which isn't safe.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I reserve a charter?

Book online above with a $100 deposit, or call, text or email Capt. Hoyt at 305-850-8898 or SandbarSomewhereCharter@gmail.com. Let him know your name, how to reach you and what kind of charter you'd like.

How many people can come?

Up to 4 guests for fishing charters, and up to 6 guests for sandbar, eco-tour, island hopping or sunset cruises.

Where do we meet?

We're based on Summerland Key in the Lower Keys. Your pick-up location is confirmed when you book.

Do I need a fishing license?

No, fishing licenses are included on all fishing trips, along with bait, rods, reels and tackle.

When can I do the Bridge Tarpon trip?

Bridge tarpon fishing runs seasonally from April through December, in the evenings only.

Is tipping expected?

A gratuity of 20% of the trip fee is customary and appreciated. It isn't included in the trip price.

Is it good for kids?

Absolutely. We're family-friendly, and guests regularly tell us how patient Captain Hoyt is with young anglers.
